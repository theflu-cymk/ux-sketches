# Sketches 007–009: design fundamentals, taught through play

Date: 2026-11-26 · Status: draft for Brian's approval

## Intent

Three more UX sketches on the shared stage, this time teaching design fundamentals by letting people play
with them: how a design system builds a screen (atomic design), how spacing works (padding, margin, gutter),
and the parts of a typeface. Same audience and bar as 001–006: fun, often a little absurd, solid craft,
works on phone and desktop, reduced-motion version, floor question and note.

## Constraints (unchanged from the series)

Vite + TypeScript + Three.js on `@ux-sketches/stage`; tested pure logic; thumbnail, `pnpm check` and the
Lab feed as before. Anti-goals: real brand assets; text-heavy lessons; anything that reads as a static
infographic.

## 007 Atomic Build (decided by Brian: the reveal is an iOS-style home screen, hidden by close-ups)

- Stages, advanced by tap / Space / a scrub slider (forward and back): **atoms → molecules → organisms →
  templates → page.**
  - Atoms: colour chips, a rounded-square tile, type samples, a dot, signal bars, a battery outline.
  - Molecules: an app icon with its label, the battery indicator, a page-dot row, a clock.
  - Organisms: the status bar, a 4×4 icon grid, the dock, a widget.
  - Template: the grid, dock and status bar as grey wireframe blocks in a tall rounded frame.
  - Page: colour, wallpaper and icons fill in.
- **Camera (Brian):** tight, close-up shots on each piece as it forms, so the whole is never in view. Each
  stage cuts or glides to a new macro framing; only at the final stage does the camera pull back and the
  frame reads as a phone's home screen. Reduced motion: hard cuts between framings, no dolly.
- The floor label names the current stage ("ATOMS", "MOLECULES", …) alongside the usual heading.
- **Brand (Claude's call):** iOS-*style*, built from original parts: no Apple logo, no real app icons
  (original simple glyph icons), no Apple fonts (IBM Plex as everywhere else).

## 008 Box Model (Claude's pick, not objected to)

- A row of cards on the stage, each literally a box inside a box.
  - **Padding** is soft foam inside the card's walls: drag a handle and it thickens, squeezing the content.
  - **Margin** is a force field outside the card: grow it and neighbouring cards get pushed away.
  - **Gutter** is the channel between cards: widen it and a stream of little marbles rolls through the gap.
- Live CSS on the floor updates as you go (`padding: 24px; margin: 16px; gap: 16px;`).
- Reduced motion: the same controls, with the cards settling instantly and no rolling marbles.

## 009 Type Anatomy (Claude's pick, not objected to)

- A giant specimen word (for example "Hxgfa", chosen to show every part) stands on the stage.
- Hover or tap a part (baseline, x-height, cap height, ascender, descender, stem, bowl, counter, serif,
  terminal, ear, tail) and it lifts out of the letter and names itself in a callout.
- The baseline, x-height and cap-height guides are draggable lines; drag them and the letters re-proportion
  to match, so you feel what each measure controls.
- Keyboard: Tab cycles through the parts, with the same callouts.
